Administrative district boundaries

Source: Statistics Korea SGIS Open API, collected and published by the
StatGarten/swcho korea-maps project (2020 boundaries).
https://github.com/swcho/korea-maps

The included GeoJSON was transformed from EPSG:5179 to WGS84 and simplified
for mobile map display. It contains 250 city/county/district features.
